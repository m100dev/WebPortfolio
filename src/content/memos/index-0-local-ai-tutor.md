I've been checking out [Index 0](https://www.index-0.in/), a free learning app built by Madhumitha Kolkar, a senior machine learning engineer. It covers data structures and algorithms, ML/AI, system design, electronics, and cybersecurity, and it has a built-in AI coach called Zero. What got my attention wasn't the amount of content; it was how the AI is used.

## It runs on your machine

Zero runs locally through Ollama with a Gemma model. No account, no backend server, and no telemetry. You install it, it sets up what it needs, and your practice stays on your computer. You can bring your own hosted model if you want, but the default is fully local and free.

I really like this part. I run local models on my own hardware too, and once there's no cost per question, you're more willing to stay on a hard problem instead of rushing through it.

## It doesn't hand you the answer

This is what I like most. Every DSA problem goes through six stages:

1. Framing the problem
2. Building intuition
3. A first implementation
4. Complexity analysis
5. Optimization
6. Final review

The hints get more specific as you go, but they never just give you the pattern, and no solution code shows up until the final review. Their tagline sums it up: "Zero helps you build your skills, not replace them."

Any chatbot can give you the answer. Building a tutor that holds back on purpose and walks you through the thinking is harder, and it's the right call for learning. With DSA the goal is recognizing the pattern the next time you see it, without help.

## Why I'm sharing it

I use AI as a tutor for my own studying. I ask detailed questions and work through how things connect until I'm confident I actually understand it. Index 0 builds that kind of learning right into the app, so you don't have to set it up yourself.

It's available for macOS, Windows, and Linux, and the releases are on [GitHub](https://github.com/MadhumithaKolkar/index0-releases). If you're prepping for interviews or just want your fundamentals to stick, it's definitely worth checking out.
